**THURSDAY: CHEST AND BACK**

■ Dynamic Warm-Up

*This is the [Week 1 Thursday](/workout-week1-thursday/) workout, with the rest between sets cut down to 50 seconds.*

**■ Circuit 1:** This couplet set is performed with little or no rest between each set until both exercises are completed.

### Dumbbell Flye
<img src="/exercises/dumbbell-flye.gif" alt="Dumbbell flye demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

Sets of 15, 12, 10, 10, and 10 reps; weight will be moderate, so that you can get all of the reps with good form.

### Two-Arm Bent-Over Dumbbell Row with Neutral Grip
<img src="/exercises/two-arm-bent-over-dumbbell-row.gif" alt="Two-arm bent-over dumbbell row demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

Sets of 15, 12, 10, 10, and 10 reps; weight will be moderate, so that you can get all of the reps with good form.

Rest 50 seconds and then repeat circuit until done.

**■ Circuit 2:** This couplet set is performed with little or no rest between each set until both exercises are completed.

### Close-Grip Barbell Bench Press
<img src="/exercises/close-grip-barbell-bench-press.gif" alt="Close-grip barbell bench press demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

10 to 12 reps; weight will be light to moderate, so that you can get all of the reps with good form. The close grip will be 12 inches between the hands.

### Lat Pull-Down with Narrow Supinated Grip
<img src="/exercises/lat-pulldown-supinated.gif" alt="Reverse-grip lat pull-down demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

10 to 12 reps.

Rest 50 seconds and then repeat circuit. Do this 4 times. Push yourself to stay at the same weight, or slightly increase the weight on each set.

**■ Circuit 3:** This couplet set is performed with little or no rest between each set until both exercises are completed.

### Bar Dip
<img src="/exercises/bar-dip.gif" alt="Bar dip demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

AMRAP (as many reps as possible); shoulder must go below height of elbow and must lock out arms on each rep to count.

### Low Row with Narrow Supinated Grip
<img src="/exercises/low-row.gif" alt="Low row demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

10 to 12 reps; weight will be moderate, so that you can get all of the reps with good form.

Rest 50 seconds and then repeat circuit. Do this 4 times. Push yourself to stay at the same weight, or slightly increase the weight on each set.

■ After the workout: Go directly into low- to medium-intensity cardio for at least 20 minutes.
