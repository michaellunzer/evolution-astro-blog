**FRIDAY: LEGS AND TRICEPS**

■ Dynamic Warm-Up

*This is the [Week 3 Friday](/workout-week3-friday/) workout, with the rest between sets cut down to 50 seconds.*

**■ Circuit 1:** This couplet set is performed with little or no rest between each set until both exercises are completed.

### Dead Lift
<img src="/exercises/deadlift.gif" alt="Barbell deadlift demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

15 reps; weight will be moderate, so that you can get all of the reps with good form.

### Lying J Press
*(demonstration image coming soon)*

12 reps; weight will be moderate, so that you can get all of the reps with good form.

Rest 50 seconds and then repeat circuit. Do this 4 times. Push yourself to stay at the same weight, or slightly increase the weight on each set.

**■ Circuit 2:** This triplet set is performed with little or no rest between each set until all three exercises are completed.

### Dumbbell Walking Lunge
<img src="/exercises/walking-lunge.gif" alt="Dumbbell walking lunge demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

15 steps with each leg; weight will be light to moderate, so that you can get all of the reps with good form.

### (Heavy) V-Bar Triceps Extension
<img src="/exercises/v-bar-triceps-extension.gif" alt="V-bar triceps extension demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

10 reps; weight will be heavy, but get all of the reps with good form.

### V-Bar Triceps Extension
<img src="/exercises/v-bar-triceps-extension.gif" alt="V-bar triceps extension demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

10 reps; weight will be moderate, but get all of the reps with good form.

Rest 50 seconds and then repeat circuit. Do this 4 times. Push yourself to stay at the same weight, or slightly increase the weight on each set.

**■ Circuit 3:** This triplet set is performed with little or no rest between each set until all three exercises are completed.

### Leg Extension
<img src="/exercises/leg-extension.gif" alt="Leg extension demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

12 reps; weight will be moderate, so that you can get all of the reps with good form.

### Leg Curl
<img src="/exercises/leg-curl.gif" alt="Leg curl demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

12 reps; weight will be moderate, so that you can get all of the reps with good form.

### One-Arm Dumbbell Lying Triceps Extension
<img src="/exercises/one-arm-dumbbell-lying-triceps-extension.gif" alt="One-arm dumbbell lying triceps extension demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

12 reps each arm.

Rest 50 seconds and then repeat circuit. Do this 4 times. Push yourself to stay at the same weight on each set.

■ After the workout: Go directly into low- to medium-intensity cardio for at least 20 minutes.
