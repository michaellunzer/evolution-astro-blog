**SATURDAY: DELTOIDS AND BICEPS**

■ Dynamic Warm-Up

■ Do an [Ab Program](/ab-program)

*This is the [Week 1 Saturday](/workout-week1-saturday/) workout, with the rest between sets cut down to 50 seconds.*

**■ Circuit 1:** This couplet set is performed with little or no rest between each set until both exercises are completed.

### Barbell Upright Row
<img src="/exercises/barbell-upright-row.gif" alt="Barbell upright row demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

Sets of 15, 12, 10, 10, and 10 reps; weight will be moderate, so that you can get all of the reps with good form.

### Triple Dumbbell Curl
<img src="/exercises/triple-dumbbell-curl.gif" alt="Dumbbell curl demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

Sets of 15, 12, 10, 10, and 10 reps; weight will be moderate, so that you can get all of the reps with good form.

Rest 50 seconds and then repeat circuit until done.

**■ Circuit 2:** This couplet set is performed with little or no rest between each set until both exercises are completed.

### Seated Dumbbell Overhead Press
<img src="/exercises/seated-dumbbell-overhead-press.gif" alt="Seated dumbbell overhead press demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

10 to 12 reps; weight will be light to moderate, so that you can get all of the reps with good form.

### Straight-Bar Cable Curl
<img src="/exercises/straight-bar-cable-curl.gif" alt="Straight-bar cable curl demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

10 to 12 reps.

Rest 50 seconds and then repeat circuit. Do this 4 times. Push yourself to stay at the same weight, or slightly increase the weight on each set.

**■ Circuit 3:** This couplet set is performed with little or no rest between each set until both exercises are completed.

### Dumbbell Rotating Lateral Raise
<img src="/exercises/dumbbell-rotating-lateral-raise.gif" alt="Dumbbell lateral raise demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

12 reps; weight will be moderate, so that you can get all of the reps with good form.

### Dumbbell Alternating Curl
<img src="/exercises/dumbbell-alternating-curl.gif" alt="Dumbbell alternating curl demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

20 reps, 10 each arm; weight will be moderate, so that you can get all of the reps with good form.

Rest 50 seconds and then repeat circuit. Do this 4 times. Push yourself to stay at the same weight, or slightly increase the weight on each set.
