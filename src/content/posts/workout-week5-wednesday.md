**WEDNESDAY: DELTOIDS AND BICEPS**

**Workout 1 (Preferably First Thing in the Morning on an Empty Stomach)**

■ 30 to 45 minutes of low- to medium-intensity cardio. Can be any variant that you like (stairs, treadmill, rower, jogging outside), but you must keep a consistent pace, with your heart rate in the 120-to-130 range.

**Workout 2 (Some Other Time During the Day)**

■ Dynamic Warm-Up
■ Do an Ab Program. (See page 124.)

**■ Circuit 1:** This couplet set is performed with little or no rest between each set until both exercises are completed.

### Seated Military Press (drop set)
<img src="/exercises/seated-military-press.gif" alt="Seated military press demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

5 reps; lower the weight for 10 reps and then lower the weight again for 15 reps.

### EZ-Bar Curl with Narrow Grip and with Outside Grip
<img src="/exercises/ez-bar-curl.gif" alt="EZ-bar curl demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

12 reps; switch grips from outside to inside and do 2 sets each.

Rest 1 minute and then repeat circuit. Do this 4 times. Push yourself to stay at the same weight, or slightly increase the weight on each set.

**■ Circuit 2:** This couplet set is performed with little or no rest between each set until both exercises are completed.

### Seated Rear-Deltoid Bent-Over Dumbbell Reverse Flye (drop set)
<img src="/exercises/rear-deltoid-raise.gif" alt="Dumbbell reverse flye demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

12 reps; lower the weight for 12 reps.

### Dumbbell Alternating Curl with Twist (drop set)
<img src="/exercises/dumbbell-alternating-curl.gif" alt="Dumbbell alternating curl demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

6 reps each; lower the weight for 8 reps each and then lower the weight again for 10 reps each.

Rest 1 minute and then repeat circuit. Do this 4 times. Push yourself to stay at the same weight, or slightly increase the weight on each set.

**■ Circuit 3:** This couplet set is performed with little or no rest between each set until both exercises are completed.

### Rope Front Raise
<img src="/exercises/cable-front-raise.gif" alt="Cable/rope front raise demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

12 reps.

### Rope Hammer Curl
<img src="/exercises/rope-hammer-curl.gif" alt="Rope hammer curl demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

12 reps; weight will be moderate, so that you can get all of the reps with good form.

Rest 1 minute and then repeat circuit. Do this 4 times. Push yourself to stay at the same weight, or slightly increase the weight on each set.

**■ Circuit 4:** This couplet set is performed with little or no rest between each set until both exercises are completed.

### Dumbbell Lateral Raise
<img src="/exercises/dumbbell-lateral-raise.gif" alt="Dumbbell lateral raise demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

12 reps.

### Triple Dumbbell Curl
<img src="/exercises/triple-dumbbell-curl.gif" alt="Dumbbell curl demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

12 reps each; weight will be light, so that you can get all of the reps with good form.

Rest 1 minute and then repeat circuit. Do this 4 times. Push yourself to stay at the same weight, or slightly increase the weight on each set.
