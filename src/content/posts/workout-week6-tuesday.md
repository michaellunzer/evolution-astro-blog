**TUESDAY: LEGS AND TRICEPS**

**Workout 1 (Preferably First Thing in the Morning on an Empty Stomach)**

■ 45 to 60 minutes of low- to medium-intensity cardio. Can be any variant that you like (stairs, treadmill, rower, jogging outside), but you must keep a consistent pace, with your heart rate in the 120-to-130 range.

**Workout 2 (Some Other Time During the Day)**

■ Dynamic Warm-Up

*This is the [Week 5 Tuesday](/workout-week5-tuesday/) workout, with the rest between sets cut down to 50 seconds.*

**■ Circuit 1:** This giant set is performed with little or no rest between each set until all four exercises are completed.

### Barbell Back Squat
<img src="/exercises/barbell-back-squat.gif" alt="Barbell back squat demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

10 reps; weight will be moderate, so that you can get all of the reps with good form.

### Plyo Squat Jump
<img src="/exercises/jump-squat.gif" alt="Jump squat demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

10 reps.

### Body-Weight Squat
*(demonstration image coming soon)*

10 reps.

### Diamond Push-Up
<img src="/exercises/diamond-push-up.gif" alt="Diamond push-up demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

15 reps.

Rest 50 seconds and then repeat circuit. Do this 4 times. Push yourself to stay at the same weight, or slightly increase the weight on each set.

**■ Circuit 2:** This triplet set is performed with little or no rest between each set until all three exercises are completed.

### One-Leg Leg Curl
<img src="/exercises/leg-curl.gif" alt="Leg curl demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

12 reps; start with the left leg and then go directly into the Lunge, also with the left leg. Then repeat the Leg Curl with the right leg and the Lunge, also with the right leg. When you’ve finished these 2 exercises go to the V-Bar Triceps Extension.

### One-Leg Lunge
<img src="/exercises/lunge.gif" alt="Lunge demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

12 reps each.

### V-Bar Triceps Extension (triple drop set)
<img src="/exercises/v-bar-triceps-extension.gif" alt="V-bar triceps extension demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

10 reps; lower the weight for 10 reps and then lower the weight again for 10 reps.

Rest 50 seconds and then repeat circuit. Do this 4 times. Push yourself to stay at the same weight, or slightly increase the weight on each set.

**■ Circuit 3:** This couplet set is performed with little or no rest between each set until both exercises are completed.

### Box or Bench Jump
<img src="/exercises/box-jump.gif" alt="Box jump demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

15 reps; stand up all the way each time on the Jump.

### Bench Dip
<img src="/exercises/bench-dip.gif" alt="Bench dip demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

15 reps.

Rest 50 seconds and then repeat circuit. Do this 4 times. Push yourself to stay at the same weight, or slightly increase the weight on each set.

**■ Circuit 4:** This couplet set is performed with little or no rest between each set until both exercises are completed.

### One-Leg Bench Bridge
<img src="/exercises/glute-bridge.gif" alt="Glute bridge demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

15 reps each leg.

### Burpee
<img src="/exercises/burpee.gif" alt="Burpee demonstration" width="200" loading="lazy" style="background:#fff;border-radius:8px;padding:6px" />

15 reps.

Rest 50 seconds and then repeat circuit. Do this 4 times. Push yourself to stay at the same weight, or slightly increase the weight on each set.

■ After the workout: Go directly into low- to medium-intensity cardio for at least 20 minutes.
